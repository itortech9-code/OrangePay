SOLTOR · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Draft date: October 22, 2026 · Effective date: only when a reviewed final version is published

1. Who is responsible

SOLTOR CAPITAL LIMITED is the proposed controller responsible for personal information processed through Soltor, subject to confirmation of its legal role for each service. The address supplied for this draft is Central Area, Abuja, Nigeria. For privacy requests or questions, contact kng@soltorcapital.com.

2. Information we process

Based on the current application design, Soltor may process:

  • Registration details such as your name, email address or phone number, password hash, and contact-verification status.
  • Authentication and security records, including one-time-code digests, password-reset records, session identifiers, timestamps, and audit events.
  • Information you enter in a feature, such as payment amount and email, transfer recipient and bank details, and bank-link initiation name and email. Soltor stores payment and transfer references, statuses, and masked bank account details; the raw bank details are sent to the relevant provider to perform the requested sandbox operation.
  • Bank-link metadata returned by Mono, such as provider account identifier, institution, currency, masked account number, and connection status.
  • Identity-verification consent, job identifiers, and status metadata returned by Smile ID. The hosted verification provider may collect identity and biometric information directly from you under its own notice and terms.
  • Technical information necessary to secure and operate the service. Exact hosting, infrastructure, and log fields should be confirmed before final publication.

Soltor’s current forms do not ask you to enter BVN or NIN. Smile ID provides a hosted identity-verification flow; review Smile ID’s notice before providing documents or biometric information there. Do not submit real identity documents, bank details, or funds while using this test application.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and administer accounts, verify contact details, authenticate users, recover accounts, record privacy and identity-verification consent, provide requested test features, maintain payment and bank-link status, respond to support requests, protect the service from abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. The lawful basis for each purpose will depend on the activity and must be confirmed under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law before final publication. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent subject to legal and operational limits.

4. Providers and disclosures

Depending on which integrations are enabled, information may be processed by Render (hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), the configured Redis service (rate limiting), Resend or Termii (email or SMS verification), Paystack (sandbox payments and bank transfers), Mono (bank linking), Smile ID (hosted identity verification), and Crisp (optional support chat). The browser may also request fonts from Google Fonts. Only providers configured for a feature should receive information for that feature; verify the actual production configuration before launch.

We may disclose information to a provider to carry out your request, to our professional advisers, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law or needed to protect people, rights, or the service. Provider terms and privacy notices apply to their processing. The exact provider entities, contractual roles, and recipients remain subject to verification.

5. Processing outside Nigeria

Some infrastructure and providers may process or store information outside Nigeria. The specific countries, transfer mechanisms, and safeguards have not been verified for this draft. Before production, Soltor must identify relevant locations and put in place any assessment, notice, contractual, or other safeguards required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable regulations.

6. Retention and security

Verification codes are designed to expire after 10 minutes, password-reset tokens after 15 minutes, and login sessions after 12 hours. Other account, transaction, provider-reference, consent, and audit records do not currently have a documented production retention schedule. We will need to define and publish appropriate retention periods, deletion and backup processes, and legal-hold rules before production use. We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and complaints

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to or correction of your personal information, request deletion, object to or restrict processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or another competent regulator. To make a request, email kng@soltorcapital.com and include enough information for us to understand and verify the request. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, BVN, NIN, or identity documents. We will handle requests within applicable legal time limits and may need to verify your identity or explain a lawful reason we cannot fully comply.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Soltor uses a first-party session cookie to keep you signed in and support security protections. In production it is configured as Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite=Lax, with a session lifetime of up to 12 hours. Google Fonts may receive a request from your browser when pages load. Crisp chat is loaded only after you choose to open support chat, when configured. Smile ID’s hosted verification flow may use its own technologies. Confirm the deployed cookie inventory and any consent requirements before final publication.

9. Children and updates

Soltor is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Soltor, contact us at kng@soltorcapital.com. We may update this notice as the service changes. A final notice should explain how material changes will be communicated and show its effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact SOLTOR CAPITAL LIMITED at kng@soltorcapital.com, Central Area, Abuja, Nigeria.
