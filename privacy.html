Privacy Notice

Effective date: October 22, 2026

1. Who is responsible

SOLTOR CAPITAL LIMITED is responsible for personal information processed through Soltor. Our address is Central Area, Abuja, Nigeria. For privacy requests or questions, contact kng@soltorcapital.com.

2. Information we process

Based on the current application design, Soltor may process:

  • Registration details such as your name, email address or phone number, password hash, and contact-verification status.
  • Authentication and security records, including one-time-code digests, password-reset records, session identifiers, timestamps, and audit events.
  • Information you enter in a feature, such as payment amount and email, transfer recipient and bank details, and bank-link initiation name and email. Soltor stores payment and transfer references, statuses, and masked bank account details; the raw bank details are sent to the relevant provider to perform the requested sandbox operation.
  • Bank-link metadata returned by Mono, such as provider account identifier, institution, currency, masked account number, and connection status.
  • Identity-verification consent, job identifiers, and status metadata returned by Smile ID. The hosted verification provider may collect identity and biometric information directly from you under its own notice and terms.
  • Technical information necessary to secure and operate the service, which may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

Soltor’s current forms do not ask you to enter BVN or NIN. Smile ID provides a hosted identity-verification flow; review Smile ID’s notice before providing documents or biometric information there. Do not submit real identity documents, bank details, or funds while using this test application.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and administer accounts, verify contact details, authenticate users, recover accounts, record privacy and identity-verification consent, provide requested test features, maintain payment and bank-link status, respond to support requests, protect the service from abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on the activity, we process information where necessary to provide a requested service, with your consent, for our legitimate interests, or to comply with legal obligations, as permitted by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent subject to legal and operational limits.

4. Providers and disclosures

Depending on which integrations are enabled, information may be processed by Render (hosting), MongoDB Atlas (database), the configured Redis service (rate limiting), Resend or Termii (email or SMS verification), Paystack (sandbox payments and bank transfers), Mono (bank linking), Smile ID (hosted identity verification), and Crisp (optional support chat). The browser may also request fonts from Google Fonts. Only providers configured for a feature receive information for that feature.

We may disclose information to a provider to carry out your request, to our professional advisers, or where disclosure is required or permitted by law or needed to protect people, rights, or the service. Provider terms and privacy notices apply to their processing.

5. Processing outside Nigeria

Some infrastructure and providers may process or store information outside Nigeria. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will use transfer mechanisms and safeguards required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable regulations. You may contact us for further information about the safeguards applicable to a particular transfer.

6. Retention and security

Verification codes expire after 10 minutes, password-reset tokens after 15 minutes, and login sessions after 12 hours. We retain other account, transaction, provider-reference, consent, and audit records only for as long as necessary for the purposes described here, including security, dispute handling, and compliance with legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the record type and applicable legal requirements. We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights and complaints

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access to or correction of your personal information, request deletion, object to or restrict processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or another competent regulator. To make a request, email kng@soltorcapital.com and include enough information for us to understand and verify the request. Do not email passwords, one-time codes, BVN, NIN, or identity documents. We will handle requests within applicable legal time limits and may need to verify your identity or explain a lawful reason we cannot fully comply.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Soltor uses a first-party session cookie to keep you signed in and support security protections. In production it is configured as Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite=Lax, with a session lifetime of up to 12 hours. Google Fonts may receive a request from your browser when pages load. Crisp chat is loaded only after you choose to open support chat, when configured. Smile ID’s hosted verification flow may use its own technologies.

9. Children and updates

Soltor is intended for adults and is not designed for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Soltor, contact us at kng@soltorcapital.com. We may update this Notice as the service changes. We will publish updates with a revised effective date and provide notice of material changes where required by law.

10. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, contact SOLTOR CAPITAL LIMITED at kng@soltorcapital.com, Central Area, Abuja, Nigeria.
